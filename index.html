inbox-pipeline

A personal, private automation script that reads Graeme Campbell's own Gmail inbox to triage unread email into a personal Trello board. Not a public product — single user, no data shared with or sold to third parties.

Privacy Policy

This app accesses Gmail via the read-only Gmail API scope, for the sole purpose of triaging the account owner's own inbox. Email data is processed locally and is not stored, shared, sold, or used for any purpose beyond generating a personal summary and Trello cards for the account owner. No data is transmitted to any third party other than the Trello and Anthropic APIs the account owner has configured, solely to perform this function on the owner's behalf.
